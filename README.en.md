# GIF & stickers

Your library of animated GIFs and images, ready to copy in one click and paste into Discord, WhatsApp, an email or a document. With NeoDex's stickers, favourites and collections.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Adding images

- **+ Add**: choose GIFs or images on your PC.
- **Link…**: paste a direct link (`.gif`, `.png`, `.jpg`…) or a Giphy page; the image is downloaded into your library.
- **Drag files** into the window, or **paste an image** with Ctrl+V.

## Using

- **Click** an image: it is copied. Paste it anywhere with Ctrl+V; a GIF stays animated when pasted into Discord, WhatsApp and most messaging apps.
- **★**: add to favourites; **✎**: change the name and keywords; **X**: delete (on all your PCs).
- **Search for a GIF or a sticker** filters by name and keywords.

## Organising

The left column offers:

- **All**: your whole library;
- **★ Favourites**;
- **Recent**: the last 24 images copied;
- **NeoDex stickers**: the stickers that come with NeoDex, ready to copy;
- **Collections**: each keyword you give an image (✎ button, words separated by commas) becomes a collection.

## Data and sync

Your library, favourites and keywords are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. The plugin asks for the clipboard (to copy), your documents (to add files) and the network (to download from a link).

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → GIF & stickers → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
